ParolNet

Private calls and messages, straight from your device to theirs.

No account, no phone number, no email. Nothing is stored on a server.

Open ParolNet

Add it to your device

ParolNet runs in your browser. You can also add it to your home screen or your apps, where it opens in its own window.

iPhone and iPad, in Safari
  1. Open ParolNet in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button, a square with an arrow pointing up.
  3. Tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Check that the name says ParolNet, then tap Add.

Android, in Chrome
  1. Open ParolNet in Chrome.
  2. Tap the menu, the three dots at the top right.
  3. Tap Install app or Add to Home screen.
  4. Tap Install. ParolNet appears with your other apps.

Computer, in Chrome or Edge
  1. Open ParolNet in Chrome or Edge.
  2. Click the install button at the right end of the address bar. If there is none, open the browser menu and choose Install ParolNet (in Edge: Apps, then Install this site as an app).
  3. Click Install. ParolNet opens in its own window.

Firefox

Firefox on a computer cannot install web apps. You can still use ParolNet in a tab: keep the tab open to receive messages and calls.

Before you rely on it

  • Both of you need ParolNet open at the same time for messages and calls to go through; until then, your messages wait on your device.
  • Contacts you add can see your IP address, unless you set up your own relay server in Settings.
  • Your identity lives only in this browser on this device, so clearing the browser's data deletes it.
  • This server only helps two devices find each other; it never carries or stores your messages.
